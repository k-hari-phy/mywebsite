About

I work at the interface of quantum field theory, gravitation, and quantum information. My research explores how quantum effects manifest in curved spacetime and how they can be probed using low-energy experimental platforms.

Research Interests

QFT in Curved Spacetime Relativistic Quantum Information Quantum Optics & Interferometry Low-energy Quantum Gravity Experiments Gravitational Wave Detection

Current Work

  • Photon–graviton interaction and Raman-like mechanisms
  • Novel gravitational wave detection proposals
  • Quantum effects in non-inertial frames
  • Gravity induced entanglement and decoherence
